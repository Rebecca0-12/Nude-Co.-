Our Story

A studio built on precision

Nude&Nails was founded on a simple belief — that great nails are the result of skill, patience, and the right products. No rushing. No compromises.

Our studio is a calm, intentional space where every client gets the full attention they deserve. We use only premium, long lasting products and stay ahead of every trend so you don't have to.

Whether you want something minimal and clean or bold and expressive — this is your space.
